TL Transit Time to Escobedo Assembly Plant (EAP)

Average truckload transit time from each U.S. state to Escobedo Assembly Plant, Gral. Escobedo, Nuevo León. Mileage is a 3-point average (closest, central, furthest city) routed through the Laredo, TX crossing, applying 500 miles = 1 day plus 1 day for the border crossing. Hover a state for detail.
